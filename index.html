Robot AEGIS · V4

The robot we are putting on the field.

AEGIS is the team’s latest build. It drives on mecanum wheels so it can strafe into scoring position without a long approach.

  • Latest published design: V4 Final
  • Mecanum drive
  • Built, coded, and driven by the students

Stand next to the photo. Point out the drive train. Do not claim a mechanism that is not on the robot in the room. Invite judges to the pit for the current build.

Engineering loop

Four passes, then we run it again.

  1. 01

    Design

    Ideas become 3D models so the robot fits together before it is cut.

  2. 02

    Build

    Mechanisms are built to survive a match, not just a demo on the bench.

  3. 03

    Code

    Autonomous routines and vision tell the robot what to do when no one is driving.

  4. 04

    Compete

    Match play shows what failed. The next iteration starts from that.

This is the motto in practice. Pick one real failure from this season and one change that followed it. Keep it under a minute.

BIOBUZZ · 2026–2027

Same team habit. A new game.

BIOBUZZ, presented by RTX, is this year’s FIRST Tech Challenge game. Architechs is carrying the AEGIS build process into it: design, test, and change what the match exposes.

  • Student-owned decisions, documented as the season moves
  • Outreach stays on the calendar, not after awards
  • The goal on the field is a robot the whole team can explain

If judges ask for game strategy, answer from the robot in front of them. This slide only sets the season. Do not read a scoring plan that the team has not agreed on.
